What the center means

The white cross is the declared information-arrival reference. Its zero is a convention for each observation, not a measured world location. Earth is a candidate context marker; no Earth position has been placed in this frame.

Reading boundary

X/Y/Z/T here are physical-frame names. The time-ratio drawing uses X for time, and the statistical Atlas map has arbitrary plot axes. No measured transform joins them.

Rhythm test

Choose one event stream and one clock. For ordered times, Δᵢ = tᵢ₊₁ − tᵢ and Rᵢ = Δᵢ₊₁ / Δᵢ when Δᵢ > 0. Atlas has not selected a stream or measured a rhythm coordinate.
